## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of heirloom textile craftsmanship and high-precision industrial manufacturing. It balances the editorial grace of a couture fashion publication with the utilitarian clarity required for wholesale yarn counts, loom specifications, and B2B procurement.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface must evoke an immediate sense of weight, tactile richness, and quiet prestige. It avoids ornate, kitsch ornamentation, relying instead on disciplined editorial grids, monumental typography, and deliberate white space. The feeling is that of opening a collector’s archival volume bound in unbleached tussar silk, overlaid with precise industrial drafting notes.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Modernism meets Industrial Archival**: Razor-sharp high-contrast serif headlines paired with systematic sans-serif data tables, subtle ruled dividers (reminiscent of loom heddles), and warm, material-driven backgrounds.
- **Architectural Whitespace**: Intentional negative space framing textiles as museum artifacts rather than disposable fast-fashion commodities.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural dyestuffs, unbleached warp fibers, and iron-mordanted clays. 

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base Canvas`: Warm Ivory (`#F7F4EE`) serves as the core canvas, eliminating the sterile glare of synthetic white.
  - `Elevated Surface`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved exclusively for elevated product cards, active modals, and high-focus content modules.
  - `Recessed Surface`: Soft Sand (`#D8CCB8`) provides muted contrast for technical sidebars, specification panels, and table headers.
- **Primary & Accent Colors**:
  - `Textile Forest Green` (`#31483B`): Anchors primary interactions, bespoke order CTAs, and structural accents.
  - `Warm Terracotta` (`#A65D45`): Used for secondary focal points, batch status indicators, and artisanal collection flags.
  - `Muted Gold` (`#B5965A`): Applied sparingly for metal seal emblems, certification monograms, and luxury tier tags.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy**:
  - `Deep Charcoal` (`#171717`): Editorial headlines, primary data, and borders.
  - `Muted Olive` (`#66705A`): Secondary meta labels, loom yarn ratios, and non-critical data points.

## Typography

The typography couples the romantic authority of `Playfair Display` with the structural readability of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Used exclusively for luxury brand narratives, collection titles, atelier statements, and saree provenance stories. Italic variants are reserved for traditional terminology (e.g., *Zari*, *Kadhwa*, *Kora Silk*).
- **Functional Body & Industrial Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, rhythmic, and legible across compact screens. Drives technical data tables, yarn counts (e.g., *2/120s Ne*), GSM weights, weaving density, client logistics, and interactive forms.
- **Micro Labels**: Applied in uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to echo precision manufacturing stamps and fabric yardage selvage prints.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a classic 12-column editorial grid for desktop and an adaptable 4-column framework for mobile screens.

- **Editorial Layout Principles**:
  - Asymmetric columns: 7-column image spreads paired with 5-column technical specification sheets create visual tension between high-fashion imagery and loom metrics.
  - Ruled structure: Subtle horizontal divider lines (1px stroke in `#D8CCB8` at 60% opacity) separate editorial headers from specification data.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column full-bleed visual cards, collapsible spec accordions, sticky bottom utility bar for wholesale quotes and swatch requests. Gutter is compressed to `1.5rem` with `1.25rem` outer margin.
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Expanded `4rem` outer canvas margin to preserve generous whitespace. Technical specs sit alongside hero previews in a sticky split-screen view.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic artificiality, this system uses tactile layering rather than heavy synthetic shadows.

- **Tonal Tiers over Shadows**:
  - Base layer: Warm Ivory (`#F7F4EE`).
  - Secondary containers (swatch dossiers, specification drawers): Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Accent panels (lead times, loom availability): Soft Sand (`#D8CCB8`).
- **Tactile Ambient Occlusion**:
  - Floating components (e.g., Swatch Tray, Filter Modals) employ a whisper-soft, diffused shadow tinted with warm undertones: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Loom-Line Borders**:
  - Separation is primarily achieved via crisp 1px borders in `#171717` (at 12% opacity) or `#B5965A` (at 30% opacity for certified heritage series).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language.

- All buttons, product cards, technical tables, input fields, and swatch viewports are cut with crisp `0px` border-radii.
- This sharp geometry references architectural tailoring, cutting tables, raw fabric bolts, and the structural grid of warp and weft threads.
- Subtle pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are permitted only for status micro-pills (e.g., "Active Loom", "Reserved Lot") to provide instant visual grouping against geometric content.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Atelier Order / Inquire)**: Flat Deep Charcoal (`#171717`) or Textile Forest Green (`#31483B`) background, Pure White text, 0px border radius, uppercase `label-md` typography. Hover transitions seamlessly to Muted Olive (`#66705A`).
- **Secondary (Technical Specs / Yarn Sheet)**: Transparent background, 1px Deep Charcoal border (`#171717`), charcoal text. On hover, background shifts to Soft Sand (`#D8CCB8`).
- **Tertiary (Swatches / Editorial Link)**: Text-only with an underline offset by 4px, transitioning from Charcoal to Warm Terracotta (`#A65D45`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat Warm Ivory or Pure White container, bounded by a 1px border (`rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.2)`).
- On focus: Crisp 1px outline in Textile Forest Green (`#31483B`) with no glow. Floating labels use `label-sm` in Deep Charcoal.
- Specification inputs (e.g., Meters, Warp Tension, Purity Grade) include fixed right-aligned unit indicators in Muted Olive.

### Cards & Swatch Dossiers
- **Fabric Showcase Card**: High-aspect ratio (3:4 or 4:5), 0px corners, enclosed in a 1px muted sand line. Technical details (weave type, warp/weft material, yarn count) sit directly below the visual frame in a strict two-column typographic layout.
- **Manufacturing Lot Card**: Pure White background on Warm Ivory canvas. Houses lot ID, loom operator sign-off, testing metrics, and pure metallic badge tags.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Thin horizontal rules (1px `#D8CCB8`) with no vertical grid lines.
- Left-aligned text for yarn/dye descriptions; right-aligned tabular numerals (`spec-num`) for weight (GSM), reed picks, thread counts, and lead times.
- Alternating rows avoid striped fills; contrast is maintained entirely through typography and hover states in `#F7F4EE`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Loom Status Badge**: Sharp rectangular badge with an active 6px indicator dot (Forest Green for operating, Warm Terracotta for dyed queue, Muted Gold for museum archival weave).
- **Weave Composition Bar**: A thin, segmented bar displaying blend percentages (e.g., 70% Mulberry Silk, 30% Zari Gold) using alternating fills of `#31483B`, `#B5965A`, and `#D8CCB8`.
